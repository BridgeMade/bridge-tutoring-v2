## Positioning (use verbatim or close)

- One-liner: **Other tutors teach the work. We teach your child how to learn.**
- Short form: **More than marks.**
- Parent-focused: **Built for how your child learns.**

**Formal positioning:** For parents in Johannesburg and Pretoria who want more
than a quick fix for their child's marks, Bridge is the personalised tutoring
partner that builds the learning foundations and habits students need to succeed
long-term — combining structured individual programmes, expert tutors, and
technology that keeps parents informed at every step.

## What makes Bridge different (the four pillars)

1. **Structured programmes** — not one-off sessions. Each student gets a plan with goals and milestones.
2. **Habits & foundations** — we address *how* a student learns before teaching content.
3. **Vetted tutors** — selected for subject knowledge and ability to connect with each student.
4. **Full parent visibility** — the Bridge app keeps parents informed with reports, tracking, communication.

## Purpose / vision / mission

- **Purpose:** To bridge the gap between where students are and where they're capable of going.
- **Vision:** A South Africa where every student has personalised support to reach their full potential.
- **Mission:** To give every student the right tutor, a structured learning plan, and clear progress — so parents always know their child is in good hands.

## Service model

- **Online tutoring:** available countrywide (all of South Africa).
- **In-person tutoring:** Pretoria and Johannesburg, plus the surrounding suburbs (see `lib/business.ts` → `inPersonAreas`).
- Levels served: **Grade R through Grade 12 (matric), and university / college.**
- Matching is **human / hand-picked** — Bridge is NOT a marketplace or directory. The team matches each child after the request form. Never imply self-service browsing of tutors.
